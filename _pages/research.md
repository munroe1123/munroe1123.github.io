## Job Market Paper

**Media Slant from Social Networks: Social Ties and Political News Production**

This project examines whether social connections between politicians and journalists shape political news coverage in South Korea.

## Other Projects

**American Military Broadcasting and Democratization**

This project studies the political effects of exposure to American military broadcasting during authoritarian rule in South Korea.

**Government Advertising and Local Media**

This project examines whether local governments use advertising expenditures to influence financially constrained local media outlets.
